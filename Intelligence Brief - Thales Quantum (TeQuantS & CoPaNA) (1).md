# INTELLIGENCE BRIEF — EMERGING TECH & CYBER RISK

**Subject:** Thales Quantum Announcements (TeQuantS & CoPaNA) — Overview & Cyber/Risk Implications  
**Date:** November 24, 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

In November 2026, Thales reached two structuring milestones within the European quantum ecosystem: launching laboratory testing for a space-based quantum internet (**TeQuantS**, Nov. 16) and initiating a defense-backed fault-tolerant quantum computing program (**CoPaNA**, Nov. 24). While these developments require **no short-term changes to our network architecture**, they confirm the sector's rapid industrialization toward a 2029–2030 horizon and reinforce the urgency of executing our **PQC (Post-Quantum Cryptography)** roadmaps.

## 3. Impact Assessment ("So What?")

* **Acceleration of Cryptographic Risk (HNDL):** With **CoPaNA**, the transition from *NISQ* (noisy machines with no immediate cryptographic threat) to **FTQC** (error-corrected computing capable of eventually breaking RSA/ECC) is moving out of purely academic research. The *Harvest Now, Decrypt Later* risk to our long-retention data is active today: maintaining momentum on our cryptographic inventory and migration to **NIST PQC** standards is critical.
* **No Short-Term Substitution by Quantum Networks:** **TeQuantS** primarily targets resilient government communications (complementing EuroQCI), networked sensors, and distributed computing, with commercialization still distant (2035). We should **neither wait for QKD/space-based links nor allocate immediate budget to them**: our operational priority remains software-based **crypto-agility**.
* **Anticipating European Regulatory Tightening:** The joint backing of the ESA, CNES, and the European Defence Fund establishes quantum as a European sovereignty priority. Regulators (via **DORA**, **NIS-2**, and ANSSI/ENISA) will logically shift from PQC recommendations to binding **audit and compliance requirements**.

---

## 4. Recommended Positioning

* **Short Term (0–12 months):** Continue and prioritize cryptographic inventory and PQC governance initiatives (no action required on quantum network infrastructure).
* **Medium Term (2027–2030):** Maintain active monitoring of FTQC milestones (CoPaNA / Pasqal) and evolving European regulatory requirements regarding the post-quantum transition.
